Welcome to my blog! This is the first post, a place to say hello and set expectations.

Why a blog?

Writing is one of the best ways to think clearly. Putting ideas into words forces me to understand them, and sharing them publicly means I might help someone else along the way.

The best time to start writing was years ago. The second best time is now.

What to expect

I plan to write about:

  • Things I’m building and the decisions behind them
  • Lessons learned, including the mistakes
  • Notes on books, tools and ideas I find interesting

Thanks for stopping by. If something here resonates with you, feel free to reach out.
